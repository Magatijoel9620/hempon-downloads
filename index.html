Are these apps free to download?

Downloading the APK does not itself guarantee that every feature is free. Individual products may have trials, subscriptions or usage limits. Check the relevant app for its current plan and pricing details.

Why does Android warn me about the APK?

Android applies additional caution to apps installed outside Google Play. Only proceed when you have verified that the download is from Hempon Group and understand the permissions being requested.

Can I install an update over my existing app?

Usually, yes, if the updated app has the same application ID, is signed with a compatible signing key and has a suitable higher version code. Back up important data before updating.

What if a download doesn't work?

Check your internet connection and available storage, then try again. If the problem continues, visit our main website to find contact and support information.

Can I download these apps on an iPhone?

These downloads are Android APK files. They cannot be installed directly on an iPhone or iPad.
